How It Works (No Reference Objects Needed)

  • 1. Scan Surrounding: Aim camera at trouser or surface. AR locks to the surface automatically.
  • 2. Tap Point 1 on Screen: Directly tap anywhere on the screen (e.g. left waistband) to anchor Point 1.
  • 3. Tap Point 2 on Screen: Directly tap the second point on the screen (e.g. right waistband). No need to move the phone!
  • 4. 100% Metric Accuracy: Real-world physical distance is calculated instantly in 3D coordinates (cm & inches).

Requires Android Chrome 81+ with Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), or WebXR desktop emulator.
